Players already published translations of "Sample Quest" that other players confirmed work, and the app gave you these as references: English ("Full translation" rev 3, verified, made for this version of the game).

Use them as hints, never as text to copy:
- Always translate from the original into Russian. A string's `ref` (language → that published translation, given only when it was made from this very original) shows what an ambiguous original means (is "Fire" a noun or a command?), who speaks, and how a name or term was written.
- Keep names and terms consistent with them where Russian allows. A string every reference leaves as the original is likely a name or a code the game shows as it is.
- They can have mistakes: when a reference contradicts the original or its context, follow the original.
- `get_references` gives the published translations of strings by key, or of the strings that mention a word (`search`): use it to see how a name or term was translated.
- The game files these translations changed are where the text most likely is; look there first: `data/Map001.json`.
